Where it comes from

Every letter is a skeleton of lines and arcs in _letters.js, laid down with a wobble the way a hand pulls a marker. A font has no pen, so _font.js turns each stroke into overlapping capsules and discs and writes the TrueType tables by hand — no font editor and no library anywhere in it. The same skeletons are what the plates print, which is why type set here and lettering on a plate are the same letters.
